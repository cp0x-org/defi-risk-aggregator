Methodology

RiskMosaic is an aggregation layer. It gathers what existing risk feeds publish and shows it verbatim. It produces no rating or score of its own.

No composite scoring, by charter

No single feed should be the last word on something this important; that is the logic of oracle diversity. Putting the feeds side by side, without averaging them, is the whole point. The no-score rule lives in a charter in the repository, and changing it would need written agreement from the Ethereum Foundation.

The ring & the colours

Each ring has one slice per feed, coloured on that feed's own scale: DeFiScan's Stage (0/1/2), DeFiPunk'd's slice colours, and so on. We never average them. A feed that publishes data but no good/bad rating, like a dashboard or a research note, shows as a neutral "covered". The centre stays empty on purpose, because there is no overall score.

Feed registry

Each feed is listed with its focus and methodology type (Rating / Dashboard / Monitoring / Research). Inclusions and exclusions are documented with rationale to maximise diversity of coverage methodologies.

Coverage & provenance

Every cell is labelled covered, partial, or not yet covered, so the gaps show up as data instead of disappearing into an average. Every verdict links back to its source. In this demo the feed verdicts are real and sourced, TVL is live from DefiLlama, and the governance column shows each protocol's DAO.
